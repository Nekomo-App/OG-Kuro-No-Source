## 📖 About Kuro No

Kuro No (often called Kuro TV) was a popular anime application that allowed users to browse, track, and watch anime through various providers. Over the years, it built a dedicated community and inspired several forks and alternative projects. The original repository was maintained by Deceptions and gained popularity among Android and TV users.

Unfortunately, development appears to have slowed significantly, and many users have reported broken sources, missing functionality, and a lack of updates in recent years. Community discussions suggest that the original project is no longer actively maintained.

## 🐱 About Nekomo

Nekomo (猫も, "cat too") is a community project inspired by the legacy of Kuro No and Shiro, and it has since been rebuilt from the ground up as an anime & manga client for **Android and Android TV**.

The goal of Nekomo is not only to preserve parts of what made those projects special, but also to give anime fans a polished app and a place to gather, share resources, discuss anime, and exchange information about alternative applications and related projects.

What it does today:

* 🎌 AniList, MyAnimeList and Kitsu sign-in and list sync
* 🧩 **Bring your own sources.** Nekomo ships with *no* sources installed (an optional official repository of free and legal sources is available, and you can add your own repositories). You add your own CloudStream, Zangetsu, Aniyomi or Mihon repositories and install what you want
* 📖 Built-in manga reader, per-title source chooser and Stable / Beta / Debug channels
* 🔄 In-app updates through GitHub releases, and a phone + Android TV build from one codebase

## 🌟 Legacy

Without Kuro No, projects such as Shiro, Nekomo, and various community forks may never have existed.

While the future of these projects remains uncertain, this page exists to help preserve information about the original project and the communities that formed around it.
